# Introduction
Hello, I am Sebastiano Baldocchi Byrne, studying to be an aerospace engineer.

Although my interests span many different concepts across engineering as a whole.
My favorite things in aerospace engineering specifically relate to astronautics.

After I complete my Bachelors Degree, I intend to work to expand my knowledge on more basic things before returning to school to complete a Masters in astronautics or astronautic adjacent engineering.
